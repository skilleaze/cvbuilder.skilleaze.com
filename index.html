WHY YOUR CV GETS IGNORED

Software reads your CV before a recruiter does.

Many employers screen applications with an applicant tracking system (ATS). If it can't read your CV, you can be filtered out, even when you're right for the job. Every application sent with the wrong CV is a chance you don't get back.

✕ A CV the ATS struggles with

  • Text inside images, tables or text boxes
  • Creative headings the system doesn't recognise
  • Missing the keywords from the job post
  • Duties listed with no results

✓ A Skilleaze CV

  • Real, selectable text in a clean reading order
  • Standard headings every ATS understands
  • Keyword check against the job you want
  • AI help to turn duties into achievements
